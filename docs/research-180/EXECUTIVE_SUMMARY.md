# Executive summary

**To: Mr. Timothy Ryan, University Fire Marshal and Director of Fire Protection and Emergency Services**

**Subject: Bailey Hall full-house evacuation, six-manager guidance and removable interventions**

We address Questions 2 and 4 of the CMCM brief: emergency departure of a concert audience, an initially unknown blocked exit, and temporary corridor barriers and directional LED strips. The audience remains 1,329 people including three wheelchair users. All new intervention cases use exactly six crowd managers, with the original building, stair/door capacities, movement parameters, response distributions and imperfect compliance. The elevator remains unavailable. Time runs from emergency declaration to the last person crossing the building boundary; assisted travel to street level is a separate endpoint.

We compared 84 arrangements of three staff layouts, three equal-length LED layouts or no LEDs, and seven barrier choices. Each LED layout has twelve 2 m floor strips (24 m total) carrying fixed public exit messages. Hard panels restrict existing corridor slots; ropes remain crossable. The model respects walls and panels in sign visibility. A longer training shortlist selected two plans before independent confirmation on 40 audiences each; blocked-exit and visibility sensitivities use additional audiences.

The prespecified combined plan uses six coverage posts, decision-area LED strips and W1/E1 ropes. Its mean last-person times are **230.1 s in spatial Model A**, **194.2 s in A-Lite**, and **182.6 s in aggregate Model B**. A-Lite's 95% interval is **[189.1, 199.3] s**. The mean 180-second target is not demonstrated. A-Lite with the same ropes but LEDs disabled gives 194.7 s; a small observed LED difference does not justify claiming a reliable benefit.

The models differ in how they represent individual following, groups and finite queues. We therefore report them separately. A full replay reproduced all 1,000 published main confirmation runs, and actual browser checks reproduced the historical Model A mean of 231.2475 s. This verifies computational consistency, not real-building calibration. Placement maps, all assumptions and mathematical rules are supplied in the technical documents.

We recommend treating the tested layouts as comparative candidates for a venue-reviewed study, preserving six staff and accessible egress. Measure actual sign legibility, voice reach, aisle flow and group behavior before choosing an installation. Clear guidance choreography matters; the evidence does not support a sanguine promise of under-180 evacuation. No unmeasured capacity increase or reduced audience was used to obtain a favorable result.
